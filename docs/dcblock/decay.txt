THE DECAY GATE'S PRECONDITION. A decay is a property of the signal, so an
estimate that MOVES when the observation window doubles is measuring the
window. Necessary, not sufficient -- it cannot prove an estimate right, and
it rejected two defects here. Allowance: 1.0 % across a doubling.

  The gates are measured at RENDER_S = 2.40 s, which is where the CY
  stops moving. The RS never does, so `t20_ms_pct` has NO VERDICT on the RS:
  it reaches exact silence about 25 ms in, and a 20 Hz brick wall's sinc
  spans the clip, so what the estimator integrates is its own smearing.
  WHAT BOUNDS THE RS INSTEAD: its absolute band energies (body, mid, HF),
