  centroid_pct is the >= 20 Hz centroid (CENTROID_FLOOR_HZ), not a global
  one: a global centroid rises MECHANICALLY when sub-20 Hz energy is
  removed, so the gate would read the candidate's intended effect as a
  failure. Qualified in closed form by dc_centroid_gate_qualification.py.

  attack_samp is the first arrival within 1.0 dB of the peak, not
  argmax(|x|): on a plateaued voice argmax answers a lobe RANK ORDER.

  Each allowance is read against the LARGER of itself and the instrument's
  own 1-LSB resolution for that property on that voice (--resolution). A
  gate below the resolution is marked [res-limited] wherever it is used.

  A normalised band SHARE is never a verdict; `share_rise_is_lf_removal`
  refuses one whose absolute band energy did not move.
