# The DC blocker, prototyped on cymbal and rimshot (#165)

**One blocker does not suit both subjects, and not because it harms the rimshot:
because the rimshot has no DC defect to remove.** That is #165 §1's "if not,
that is the finding" branch, and the mechanism is sharper than the verdict.

Every number below is regenerated by one command and nothing in this directory
is hand-written:

```
python3 tools/probes/dc_blocker.py --records     # rewrites every .txt here
python3 -m pytest tools/probes/dc_blocker.py tools/probes/test_dc_blocker_apparatus.py \
    tools/probes/test_dc_t20_gate_qualification.py tools/probes/test_dc_centroid_gate_qualification.py -q
```

## What the measurement says

`COUPLE_K` is deliberately not in `docs/sensitivity/registry.json` yet: nothing ships (`COUPLE_OFF`), the corner is read off C49 / R176‖R177 rather than chosen, and `cutoff.txt` already sweeps K = 8…13 against a closed-form prediction; it must be registered in #510 before anything enables a corner.

At the circuit's own corner (`COUPLE_K = 10`, 7.46 Hz, read off the BD's C49 /
R176‖R177 network), coupling on the drum bus before the output stage's clamp:

| voice | role | sub-20 Hz | preservation | verdict |
|---|---|--:|---|---|
| CY | subject | **−14.85 dB** | every declared gate inside its allowance, decay included (−0.36 %); attack is `[res-limited]` (delta +0 samples, no verdict change) | improvement MET, preservation ok |
| RS | subject | −2.70 dB | ok on the six gates that resolve; decay **REFUSED** | improvement **NOT MET** (required 6.0 dB) |
| BD | control | −3.25 dB | ok | ok |
| HT | control | −2.57 dB | ok | ok |
| CH | control | −5.03 dB | ok | ok |

`docs/dcblock/measure-bus.txt` is that table with its diagnostics;
`limits.txt` is the allowances, declared in the commit that added the probe,
before the first measurement.

**Why the two subjects differ.** `screen.txt` answers it before anything is
rendered. β is the share of a voice's sub-20 Hz energy *below the blocker's
corner* — the part the filter attenuates:

| | CY | CH | BD | RS | HT |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| β (share below fc) | **0.983** | 0.973 | 0.455 | 0.380 | 0.359 |
| steady-state bound, dB | 14.93 | 11.66 | 3.34 | 2.71 | 2.58 |
| measured, dB | 14.85 | 5.03 | 3.25 | 2.70 | 2.57 |

The cymbal's sub-20 Hz energy is a slowly varying offset that lives below the
corner; the rimshot's is the Fourier content of a ten-millisecond burst, which
straddles it. A DC-nulling filter removes the first and passes the second, and
no corner the hardware can build (K is a shift) reaches 6 dB on the rimshot
without moving fc into the band whose preservation is the constraint —
`cutoff.txt` prices every corner.
<!-- claim: test=tools/probes/dc_blocker.py::test_the_screen_separates_the_two_subjects_and_says_why -->

So **#152's "one DC block fixes five voices" is refuted for at least one of the
five**, and the screen classifies the rest without rendering them: BD, HT and RS
are skirt; CY and CH are below the corner.

## §2 Placement: measured, and the DC reading cannot choose it

`placement.txt` measures four placements against the same baseline.
`exc` (before the envelope) fails outright — it *raises* the BD's sub-20 Hz
energy by 5.10 dB and its peak by 1.17 dB. `bus` and `post` both pass.

`clipping.txt` is why `bus` ships, and it needed a condition that did not exist:
**at the reference gain nothing rails on any voice, so `bus` and `post` agree to
a hundredth of a dB and §2's argument is untested there.** On a five-voice stack
at full bus gain the clamp fires, and then:

- both placements remove the same sub-20 Hz energy (−12.36 vs −12.45 dB) — **a
  blocker in the wrong place still reduces the DC reading**, which is exactly
  §2's warning, measured rather than repeated;
- `bus` rails 18 samples against `post`'s 25 and lands 1.21 dB closer to the
  unclamped ideal, because removing the standing offset before the clamp buys
  back the headroom the offset was consuming.

The reference in that comparison is biased *towards* `post` (it is what `post`
computes when nothing rails), which is stated in the record. The conclusion
survives a reference that favours the loser.

## §4 The band-share trap: checked, and nothing was synthesised

Removing low-frequency energy raises a normalised high-band percentage without
adding one sample of high-frequency content. The cymbal's HF *share* rises
87.13 % → 91.50 % under coupling while its absolute 5–20 kHz energy moves
**+0.00 dB**. `share_rise_is_lf_removal` is the rule that separates the two, and
every share in `measure-*.txt` is labelled by it. No verdict in this directory
is read off a share.
<!-- claim: test=tools/probes/dc_blocker.py::test_band_energy_is_absolute_and_a_share_is_not -->
The same rule must *not* fire when harmonics really are added, which is the
control that makes it a discriminator rather than a filter.
<!-- claim: test=tools/probes/dc_blocker.py::test_the_trap_rule_does_not_fire_on_real_new_harmonics -->

**The tom warning in §4 holds: nothing here improves the toms.** HT is a
control, its absolute HF energy is unchanged, and its band-share movement is
labelled LF removal. The toms' high-band deficit is a separate,
non-excitation question — it belongs to #334, and **a DC blocker synthesises no
harmonics**. Any future tom "improvement" attributed to this change should be
treated as suspect until its absolute HF energy is shown to have risen.

## §5 Continuous playing

`continuous.txt`: eight repeated hits on each subject, an overlap, a choke, and a
retune mid-ring on each of the five exclusive-circuit pairs (LC/LT, MC/MT,
HC/HT, CL/RS, MA/CP). The blocker's state is carried across a retune rather than
cleared — a capacitor does not know a stop fired. The coupled switch step may
exceed the uncoupled one by at most `SWITCH_STEP_TOL_LSB` = 2 LSB (one LSB of
truncation per blocker, two blockers on the bus — the bound
`test_a_bus_blocker_is_the_superposition_of_per_path_blockers` asserts), and
`test_the_coupled_switch_step_does_not_exceed_the_uncoupled_by_the_truncation_bound`
asserts it. The worst observed excess is CP→MA at +1.00 LSB (the review's 1.02 was read off the six-digit column); the other four pairs
are at or below the uncoupled step. `continuous.txt` prints the excess column.

## The apparatus, which was wrong twice before it was right

Two preservation gates were measuring their own artefacts, and in both cases the
gate condemned the candidate's *intended effect*:

1. **The centroid gate** was global. Removing sub-20 Hz energy raises a global
   centroid mechanically: CY +8.96 % against a 1 % allowance while the audible
   band moved −0.02 %. Qualified in closed form by
   `dc_centroid_gate_qualification.py` and now read ≥ 20 Hz.
2. **The decay gate** was global for the same reason — T20 is a backward *energy*
   integral, so a sub-20 Hz pedestal inflates it most where the gate reads.
   The cymbal's recorded −6.96 % "decay loss" was that pedestal. Qualified by
   `dc_t20_gate_qualification.py`: a tone whose T20 is 200.0 ms by construction
   reads 452 ms globally and 202 ms banded.
   <!-- claim: test=tools/probes/test_dc_t20_gate_qualification.py::test_a_global_t20_is_inflated_by_a_harmless_sub20_pedestal -->

Neither repair is a loosened gate: the qualification feeds the repaired
estimator a genuinely 10 % faster decay and requires it to stay red.
<!-- claim: test=tools/probes/test_dc_t20_gate_qualification.py::test_the_repaired_gate_still_sees_a_real_decay_regression -->

And the repair had a precondition of its own, which the instrument failed on two
voices for two different reasons. `decay.txt`: **a decay does not depend on how
long you watched.**

- The cymbal's T20 reads 428 / 540 / 558 / 558 ms at 0.60 / 1.20 / 2.40 / 4.80 s
  — Schroeder truncation. Every record taken at 0.60 s was biased 23 % short, so
  `RENDER_S` is now 2.40 s, where it stops moving.
- The rimshot's banded T20 reads 598 / 1198 / 2398 ms at those clips: the clip's
  length to three figures. A 20 Hz brick wall's kernel spans the clip and the
  rimshot reaches exact silence about 25 ms in, so the estimator integrates its
  own smearing. **That gate is REFUSED on the rimshot** — a third outcome,
  excluded from the failure list and printed in every table, because calling it
  a preservation failure would be inventing a result. What bounds the rimshot
  instead is six gates that do resolve.
  <!-- claim: test=tools/probes/test_dc_blocker_apparatus.py::test_the_decay_gate_refuses_the_rimshot_rather_than_answering -->

A claim this directory used to make and no longer does: that the rimshot's decay
tripled (+225 %) under coupling and that this was a preservation failure. The
+225 % is real and clip-invariant, but it is the blocker's own 21 ms **sub-20 Hz**
undershoot tail — the quantity the sub20 column reports — not an audible decay
change, and the audible-band gate has no verdict on that voice at all.

One statistic was withdrawn too. The screen's class discriminator used to be φ,
the share of sub-20 Hz energy in the f = 0 bin. That bin is 1/T wide, so φ is a
property of the analysis window: the cymbal's φ fell 0.888 → 0.391 when the clip
grew, and a `φ > 0.5` label reclassified the cymbal with nothing about the
cymbal having changed. β is window-stable across the same change (0.991 →
0.983) and separates the classes by more than a factor of two at either window.

**Wrong-then-right rate for this work: five.** Four are above (the centroid
gate, the decay gate, the clip length, φ). The fifth is in the qualification
itself — its carrier's τ was written `T20/ln(100)`, so its constructed truth was
100 ms while the file claimed 200, and `main()` REFUSED rather than reporting.
All five were caught by controls or by an asserted precondition, none by
inspection.

## What is NOT done, and why

**§6 — model → RTL → decoded I²S — was not performed, and no candidate is
enabled.** `model/drums_fx.py` defaults to `COUPLE_OFF`; nothing in the shipping
path changed.

§6's own precondition is "a successful candidate", and §3's bar for a candidate
is improvement *and* preservation on **CY and RS together**. No candidate meets
that: the rimshot's 6 dB is unreachable at any buildable corner, for a reason
that is a property of the voice rather than of the filter. So the bounded
prototype's question is answered in the negative and the carry-through is not
triggered.

The cymbal-only coupling *does* clear every declared gate, and shipping it is
real work with its own verifiable deliverables — a register-contract decision
(enable bit, whether K is a field), the RTL stage, bit-exactness against the
model, then the I²S decode through `fpga/verify_fixture.py`. That is **#510**, filed rather than
smuggled in here.
