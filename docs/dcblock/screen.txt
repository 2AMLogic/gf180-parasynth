THE SCREEN. phi is the share of a voice's sub-20 Hz energy in the f = 0 bin --
the part a zero at z = 1 removes exactly. The rest is the onset envelope's own
skirt, which the blocker only attenuates by |H(f)|. So

    attenuation >= -10 log10(1 - phi)   (floor, from the DC bin alone)

and 6 dB from the DC bin alone needs phi >= 0.749. FLOOR and STEADY are both
computed from the uncoupled spectrum and the filter's own transfer function,
with NOTHING rendered; MEASURED is the integer filter run inside the block.
STEADY is an UPPER bound: a causal filter starting from rest answers the
clip's opening step with a tail of its own, and that tail is sub-20 Hz energy
too -- the same 21 ms tail that triples the rimshot's decay. MEASURED must
land between the two, which is a two-sided check of a model against an
implementation neither was fitted to.

  'K for 6 dB' is the LOWEST corner (largest K) that reaches the
  required improvement. K is a shift, so these are the only corners the
  hardware can build, and the circuit's own is K = 10 (7.46 Hz). A voice whose
  answer is a SMALLER K than that is asking for a corner the circuit does not
  have, and --cutoff is where what that costs is measured.
