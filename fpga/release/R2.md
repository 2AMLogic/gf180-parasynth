(`fpga/release/R1.md`, `r1-2025.1.json`) stays the published image and the

| `polyblep_saw_pair.v` under `VOICE_PULSE_2X`: rectangles × 24248/32768 (0.74), saw unchanged (27853); the same in `voice_fx._render_2x` | at 0.85 every rectangle saturated the 2x decimator near the top of the range; 0.74 was chosen by a rule frozen in advance: zero clipped samples, ≥ 0.1 dB margin (recheck-333 item 5) | sound owner (value); integration (RTL/model) |
